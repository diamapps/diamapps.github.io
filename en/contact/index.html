Contact

Let’s talk

Do you have a question about one of the apps, some feedback, a bug you have run into or an idea we have not had yet? Then please write to us. There is no call centre behind DiamApps, just a very small workshop – your message is read and answered personally.

There is exactly one way to reach us, and that is deliberate: email. We do without a contact form so that your message does not travel through a service provider’s servers before it reaches us.

Ways to get in touch

How to reach us

Who answers

No queue and no ticketing system: there is one person behind DiamApps who reads every message personally. Please allow a few days – in return you get an answer that actually fits your question.

A question about an app?

For questions about a particular app, the support page is often the faster route. It answers the questions that reach us most often – around the clock and without any waiting.

What happens to your data

We use what you tell us solely to answer your enquiry. All the details are set out in our privacy policy.

  • Which app is it about – DiamVAT, DiamLaw or DiamMath?

  • For a bug: what did you do, what happened, and what had you expected?

Your message goes straight into our mailbox at IONOS SE in Germany – no form or delivery service in between, no account, no advertising. What happens to your message and what rights you have is explained in our privacy policy.
