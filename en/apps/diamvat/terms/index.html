2. What DiamVAT does

DiamVAT validates VAT ID numbers via the official interfaces of the German Federal Central Tax Office and the European Commission (VIES), documents the results and prepares the EC sales list, the advance VAT return, the filing extension, the annual VAT return and messages to the tax office following the official forms. On your instruction DiamVAT submits these reports to the tax administration with your own ELSTER certificate. DiamVAT is a tool: it does not provide assistance in tax matters within the meaning of the German Tax Advisers Act and does not replace tax advice.

Only submit for companies for which you are authorised to do so, and only with a certificate that belongs to you or that you are entitled to use. Keep the certificate file and the PIN safe.

Allow for a margin before deadlines; filing via “My ELSTER” remains possible at any time. The tax administration’s transfer ticket is decisive for a filing.
